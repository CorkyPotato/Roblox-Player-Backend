for SaintNova Executor. It is derived from the private Aether workspace (2026).

The shipped Studio executor, the core, the protocol, the UI, and the tests are
in [SaintNova-Roblox-Executor](https://github.com/CorkyPotato/SaintNova-Roblox-Executor).

experiments, addresses, dumps, or bypass code. Those remain private.
